\textbf{Re: Engineering Co-op -- Civil West}

Dear Aecon Hiring Team,

I am a Structural Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University applying for a 12-month Engineering Co-op with Aecon's Civil West division, with Toronto, Ontario as my preferred location. My academic experience in surveying, AutoCAD drafting, and materials testing, together with fabrication on TMU's Concrete Canoe team, has developed my interest in construction. I am eager to support field engineers and superintendents while learning how heavy civil projects are delivered.

In my topographic surveying project, I collected total-station elevation, angle, and distance measurements, calculated traverse components, checked closure, and applied coordinate adjustments. Our work recorded a benchmark misclosure of 0.0032 m and included direct/reverse observations and differential-levelling checks. This experience gave me practice gathering field information and checking measurement accuracy, both relevant to supporting site teams.

My structural mechanics projects involved producing AutoCAD drawings for aluminum, timber, and composite specimens and performing hand and Excel calculations for stress, deflection, truss forces, and torsion. I compared theoretical predictions with laboratory measurements to evaluate the results. In geotechnical laboratories, I classified soils and evaluated compaction, permeability, consolidation, and strength. These projects provide a foundation for learning to review construction drawings, track quantities, and prepare project reports under supervision.

As a construction team member on TMU's Concrete Canoe team, I fabricate components through precision cutting, sanding, taping, and assembly. I also test alternative materials and fabrication methods, document prototype results, and collaborate on design improvements. Alongside my studies, I work as a Grocery Supervisor at Groupe Adonis, supervising two to six employees, coordinating daily tasks, training new hires, and managing inventory. Resolving customer and operational issues has strengthened my communication, organization, and adaptability.

Aecon's emphasis on safety, accountability, and teamwork appeals to me. I would bring a careful approach to my work and a willingness to learn site procedures from experienced staff. The opportunity to develop practical skills in construction coordination, progress tracking, and cost reporting is particularly valuable to me. Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background could contribute to your Civil West team.

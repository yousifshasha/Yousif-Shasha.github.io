PROJECT 04 · CVL 320 + CVL 420 · STRUCTURAL MECHANICS

Draw it.
Load it.
Explain it.

This portfolio presents six structural mechanics experiments completed in CVL 320 and CVL 420. The projects investigated stress concentration, compression, truss-member forces, beam deflection, flexural stress, and torsion using aluminum, timber, and composite specimens. Each specimen was documented with dimensioned AutoCAD drawings, analyzed through hand calculations, processed in Excel, and evaluated by comparing theoretical results with strain-gauge, dial-gauge, load-cell, and data-acquisition measurements.

ENGINEERING DEVELOPMENT

The drawing is the first calculation.

Across all six experiments, the most important habit was maintaining a traceable chain from geometry and material, to idealized mechanics, to spreadsheet formulas, to measured behaviour. The discrepancies are not failures; they show exactly where alignment, contact, material variability, gauge placement, and model assumptions influence structural response.

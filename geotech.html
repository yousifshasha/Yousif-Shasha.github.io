Material identification

Atterberg limits and sieve analysis establish the consistency and particle-size characteristics used to classify soil behaviour.

INTERPRETATION

Moderate plasticity range.

The plastic-limit trials were tightly grouped: 13.33%, 13.33%, and 13.46%. The flow curve estimated the liquid limit at 28.90%.

EXPERIMENT 02

Compaction window

Standard Proctor compaction identifies the water content range that produces the greatest dry unit weight for the tested soil.

FIELD TAKEAWAY

Add enough water to lubricate, not float.

Dry unit weight rose from 19.03 kN/m³ at 3.73% water content to 19.54 kN/m³ at about 6-7%. Beyond optimum, water occupied void space and it fell to 18.31 kN/m³.

EXPERIMENT 04

Settlement under load

An oedometer test tracked void-ratio change and the time-dependent settlement response of a fine-grained soil.

Void ratio: 0.95 → 0.63 as effective stress increased.

INTERPRETATION

Overconsolidated, moderately compressible soil.

The preconsolidation pressure of 215.44 kPa exceeded the estimated in-situ effective stress of 92 kPa, giving an OCR of 2.34.
